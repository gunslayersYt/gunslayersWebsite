01 · About

More than just a gaming channel.

Hey, I’m Josh — a university student studying Games Design & Development, a content creator, and a passionate gamer.

Gaming has been a huge part of my life for as long as I can remember. I created gunslayers to share that passion, create entertaining content, and build a community where people can come together around the games they love.

I’m also autistic and have ADHD. I want this community to be a welcoming place where people can be themselves, meet other gamers, share their experiences, and enjoy the journey together.

🏆

Trophy Hunting

I love chasing Platinum Trophies and taking games all the way to 100% completion. Every difficult trophy is another challenge to overcome.

🎮

Every Platform

PlayStation, Xbox, PC and Nintendo. I enjoy experiencing games across platforms and discovering something new to play.

🎥

Content Creation

Streams, videos, short-form clips and community content — all with the goal of making every piece better than the last.

02 · The Journey

What gunslayers is about.

The goal is simple: keep improving, keep playing, and build something that people genuinely want to be part of.

Play the games I love

From first playthroughs and story-driven adventures to multiplayer sessions, challenges and completely unexpected gaming moments.

Chase the Platinum

Find the collectibles, complete the challenges and push through the grind until that final Platinum Trophy pops.

Create and improve

Turn those experiences into streams, YouTube videos and short-form content while constantly improving the quality of the channel.

Build the community

Create a positive place where gamers can hang out, talk about what they’re playing and become part of the Gunslayers journey.
